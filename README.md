# Cyber After 40

A beginner's journey into cybersecurity and AI in my 40s: home lab projects, troubleshooting, and lessons learned.

**Learn. Build. Break. Fix. Repeat.**

I'm Mykee, a Marine Corps veteran, husband, dad, and cybersecurity student. I'm changing careers around work, college, and family, and documenting what I build, what breaks, and what I figure out along the way. I'm not an expert. This is the learning process, in public.
